## "We Need Abortions" by Quez2RR (ft. Enphamus)

I originally was going to with something more recently released today, but when I heard this song again when looking for something worthy of song of the day, I knew I had to give these guys from my city some attention. It's not an overly inventive song, but it stands out in how incredibly solid it is. Hard is one thing; it's not really that challenging for any artist to make a hard song. But it's another thing to make something solid: undeniable effort, no weaknesses, and clear success. Quez's delivers the entirety of his verse and hook consistently with his signature flow, emphatic vocals, and honestly impressive breath control given that he doesn't seem like he's punching in much. Whether someone's flow and enunciation is good or not is generally subjective, but it's objective that people definitely underestimate the amount of effort and skill an artist has to possess to slide like that over a beat for even just a minute straight. And as usual, Enphamus (the effective Patrick to Q5's SpongeBob (because Enphamus is large, not because he's dumb)) delivers the exact same amount of effort on his verse. Normally on any given song with a feat, one verse is better than the other. But on this track, with how hard Quez goes, and how naturally Enphamus matches his energy and flow, its really impossible to say either of these guys, both of which whose discographies show incredible versality, did better than the other. Overall, insanely solid song.
