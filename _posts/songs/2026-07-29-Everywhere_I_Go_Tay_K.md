## "Everywhere I Go" by Tay-K

  As many know, Tay-K got locked and he's definitely not coming back home anytime soon. Obviously he shouldn't have done what he did (blah blah murder is pretty bad), but much like when they locked up R Kelly, the music's just too good to see this guy go (don't read into that comparison). It's not hyperbole to say that Tay-K was, and as "Everywhere I Go" and his other post-lockup release "Erupt" (honorary song of the day due to it's recording in jail instead of pre-lockup), is, an incredibly unique artist. I can't say I've heard every dude out there, but I've never seen anyone come close to emulating anywhere near Tay-K's flow, cadence, or his voice (excluding early Comethazine (now Frank Kole). Much like King Von, this guy would have undoubtedly had huge, massive, mainstream influence and attention from the media if not for heir untimely lifelong incarceration (in Tay-K's case, in John B. Connally Unit in Karnes County, Texas, in Von's likely hell). Everytime I hear Tay-K's music, which is a lot because "Everywhere I Go" is ridiculously and unfathomably hard, I genuinely just have to stop and ponder what the game would be like now if Tay-K had his chance to grow as an artist and capitalize on his alredy garnered clout.  <u> TLDR: This is hard, free Tay-K (but only into a studio then take him back when he's done) </u>
