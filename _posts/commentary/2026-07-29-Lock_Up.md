blurb_text: "If you as an artist know that youre going to jail for a span of time thats not really that long in the grand scheme of things (anything around than the minimum time to warrant the release of a First Day Out track within the first week of your release), or are on trial for something you definitely did (or possibly did but your lawyer isnt great), you should prepare accordingly. Im not saying you should record a whole album to be released while you in jail, but at least an amount of songs proportional to the time you plan on spending in the pen. Obviously, even for two dudes who did the same thing in the same way, one can stay in there for half a generation and the other can get out by Christmas, so its not really that straightforward. But if you think about it, a lot of artists who be dying have whole series of posthumous releases dropped well after they in the ground. Von had What It Means To Be King and Grandson, X had Skins and Bad Vibes Forever, and Juice had Legends Never Die, Fighting Demons, and The Party Never Ends. So really, how you a dead nigga got better planning than you do and you live? Most folk (excluding purposeful suicides) dont know for a fact they boutta die, and yet when they do, they have something to drop. And if you, an artist who boutta go to jail, know you about to go jail, it really should be expected for you to record something for you label or your people to drop on your behalf, or at least have a guy boof your phone up his butt so you can drop something yourself. For the fourth time, obviously life isnt really that simple, but nobody can deny that these folks with nothing to do except attend meetings with their court appointed lawyer just be sitting around instead of ensuring they career on track for when they get out. Im not always on top of stuff, and even then if I was in the streets Id have to be pretty dumb to have a public blog, but if I got some serious traction as an artist, I think Id probably make sure I got a couple reserve releases in the tank in case something happen to me, especially if I know somethings about to happen."
